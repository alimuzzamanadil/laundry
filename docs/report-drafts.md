# 📝 Laundry System - Project Specification & Drafts

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional laundry management suffers from inefficient scheduling, lack of visibility into washing/ironing progress, manual customer records, and uncoordinated pickup/delivery routing. 

## 2. Objectives
- Provide customers with an on-demand service catalog, instant order booking, and real-time status tracking.
- Equip administrative staff with complete oversight of incoming orders, revenue statistics, and staff management.
- Provide delivery agents with focused task lists for pickup and dropoff.

## 3. User Roles & Capabilities
1. **Customer**:
   - Browse catalog with unit prices.
   - Schedule pickup date and address.
   - Track order through visual status timeline (Placed, Picked Up, Washing, Ironing, Out for Delivery, Delivered).
   - Review past order receipts.
2. **Delivery Staff**:
   - View assigned pickups and deliveries.
   - Mark orders as picked up or delivered.
3. **Admin**:
   - Service catalog configuration (prices, services).
   - Order management and manual status updates.
   - Assign staff to orders.
   - View business KPIs on admin dashboard.
